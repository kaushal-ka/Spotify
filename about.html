About Me

Hi! I'm Kaushal Karn, a passionate developer from Nepal studying BSc CSIT. This Spotify Clone is a personal project built to sharpen my frontend and UI/UX skills. I’ve poured my heart into every detail—from song interactions to responsiveness.

About the Project

Inspired by the clean design of Spotify, this clone showcases my abilities in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It features full music controls, animated UI, and responsive design. It’s not just a clone—it’s my creative interpretation.

Tech Stack Used

  • HTML5
  • CSS3 (with modern Flexbox and Grid)
  • JavaScript (vanilla, DOM manipulation)
  • Font Awesome for icons
  • LocalStorage for persistence
  • Service Workers (PWA)

Pro Tips

Volume Control: Use your mouse wheel or dragging on the volume slider for precise control
Progress Bar: Click anywhere on the progress bar to jump to that time in the song
Search: Start typing to instantly search and filter songs by name
Liked Songs: All your favorite songs are automatically saved in your browser
Dark Mode: Your theme preference is remembered between sessions
Offline Mode: The app caches songs for offline playback (PWA enabled)
