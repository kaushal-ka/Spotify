# 🎯 Quick Start Guide - New Features

## Getting Started

### 1. **Hard Refresh Browser** (Important!)

- **Windows/Linux**: Press `Ctrl + Shift + R`
- **Mac**: Press `Cmd + Shift + R`
- This clears cache and loads all new features

### 2. **Explore the Sidebar**

- **Desktop**: See sidebar on left with 5 tabs
- **Mobile**: Click ☰ button to toggle sidebar
- Tabs: Queue | History | Lyrics | Stats | Playlists

### 3. **Play a Song**

- Click any song in the list
- Song plays automatically with new auto-play feature
- Watch the purple visualizer animate!

## 🔥 Top 5 Must-Try Features

### 1. Audio Visualizer 🎨

- Look at the animated purple bars at bottom
- They pulse with your music in real-time

### 2. Statistics Dashboard 📊

- Click **Stats** tab in sidebar
- See your listening stats:

  - Liked songs count
  - Time spent listening
  - Top 3 most-played songs

### 3. Lyrics Display 📜

- Click **Lyrics** tab
- See lyrics for current song

- Share to Twitter, Facebook, WhatsApp

- Playlists save automatically!

## 🛠️ Equalizer Quick Guide

### Access EQ

1. Click **🎛️ Sliders** button in controls
2. Panel opens on bottom-right
3. Choose preset or adjust manually

### EQ Presets

- **Normal** - Flat, balanced sound
- **Bass** - Enhanced low frequencies (boom)
- **Treble** - Enhanced high frequencies (crispness)
- **Vocal** - Optimized for vocals

### Manual Adjustment

- 5 frequency bands: 60Hz, 250Hz, 1kHz, 4kHz, 12kHz
- Drag sliders left (reduce) or right (boost)
- Changes apply in real-time

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### Features not showing?

1. **Hard refresh**: `Ctrl+Shift+R`
2. **Check browser**: Must be modern (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
3. **Clear cache**: Settings → Clear browsing data

### Stats not saving?

1. Check if localStorage is enabled
2. Try incognito/private mode
3. Check browser storage limit

### Visualizer not animating?

1. Play a song first
2. Check browser console (F12) for errors
3. Refresh page if stuck

### Mini player missing?

1. It appears in bottom-right when playing
2. Click X to close (it hides, not disabled)
3. Play another song to show again
